FAQ

The practical answers for mining, IMD payment, reveals, trading and the live contract.

How many NFTs are there?

1,111 total. The contract tracks minted and remaining supply on-chain.

How does mining work?

You find a SHA-256 nonce that satisfies the current difficulty for the current round. The work runs in the browser with CPU or WebGPU support.

What do I pay with?

Mint price is denominated in IMD and is read from the active phase in the contract. Ethereum gas is separate.

Is there a private mint key?

No. The site prepares the transaction and your wallet signs it. The NFT contract enforces proof validity.

Does the artwork reveal later?

No. Artwork assignment happens as part of the successful mint settlement. The resulting tokenURI points to the matching revealed metadata.

Can I trade on OpenSea?

Yes. The site includes OpenSea links, and successful mints open the corresponding OpenSea asset URL after confirmation.

Where is the collection metadata?

The reveal metadata and artwork are published to IPFS. The deployed contract uses the revealed metadata base URI.

What if my wallet is on another network?

The mint terminal requires Ethereum Mainnet (chain ID 1). Switch networks in your wallet before mining or minting.
