HOW IT WORKS

The browser does the work. The wallet authorizes the payment. The contract decides what is valid.

OPEN MINT TERMINAL ↗
01

MINE

The current block hash, token number, wallet address and phase difficulty form the proof-of-work input. Run SHA-256 locally with CPU workers or WebGPU.

02

VERIFY

The browser checks the leading-zero difficulty and recomputes the proof hash before sending anything on-chain.

03

PAY + MINT

The current IMD price is read from the contract. When needed, the site asks for an ERC-20 approval, validates the proof on-chain, then submits the mint transaction.

04

REVEAL

After confirmation, the contract assigns a unique artwork ID. The tokenURI points at the revealed IPFS metadata for that artwork.
